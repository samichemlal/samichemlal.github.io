About me

I am an engineering student in Applied Mathematics, Machine Learning & Statistics at CentraleSupélec (MSc-equivalent, 2023–2027), after preparatory classes (MPSI–PSI*) at Lycée du Parc in Lyon.

From March to August 2026 I was a research intern at CMAP, École Polytechnique, supervised by Rémi Flamary, Karim Lounici and Thibaut Germain. Publications

Preprints and submissions. Names in bold are me.

DOODL

Geometric Dictionary Learning of Dynamical Systems with Optimal Transport

T. Germain, S. Chemlal, R. Flamary, V. R. Kostic, K. Lounici

arXiv:2605.18276, 2026 · Submitted to ICLR 2027

Related dynamical systems lie near a low-dimensional manifold in spectral operator space. DOODL learns a dictionary of characteristic spectral dynamics with Riemannian optimization and optimal transport, and estimates a new system from short trajectories. In low-data regimes it gives one to two orders of magnitude lower operator-estimation error than estimating each system independently.

arXiv PDF
MTL-CMO

Multi-Task Learning of Conditional Mean Operators: Applications to Dynamical Systems and Uncertainty Quantification

S. Chemlal, T. Germain, R. Flamary, V. R. Kostic, K. Lounici

Preprint forthcoming · Submitted to ICLR 2027

A multi-task operator-learning framework for populations of conditional distributions. It learns a representation shared across related tasks and transfers in closed form to a new distribution from limited data, with statistical guarantees. Applications include metastable Langevin dynamics and turbulent plasma simulations.

Related research to understand my papers

Two papers by my co-authors that my work builds on directly.

NCP

Neural Conditional Probability for Uncertainty Quantification

V. R. Kostic, K. Lounici, G. Pacreau, G. Turri, P. Novelli, M. Pontil

NeurIPS 2024

Read it for MTL-CMO. NCP learns conditional distributions through the conditional expectation operator: after a single training phase, it gives conditional means, quantiles and confidence regions for any conditioning. MTL-CMO extends this operator view to many related tasks at once.

arXiv NeurIPS
S-GOT

A Spectral-Grassmann Wasserstein Metric for Operator Representations of Dynamical Systems

T. Germain, R. Flamary, V. R. Kostic, K. Lounici

arXiv:2509.24920, 2025

Read it for DOODL. It represents each dynamical system as a distribution over its Koopman/transfer-operator eigenvalues and spectral projectors, and compares systems with optimal transport. This is the geometry in which DOODL learns its dictionary.
